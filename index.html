Professional Fire Door Surveys

We provide detailed fire-door inspections for commercial, residential, and healthcare buildings across London and surrounding counties. Our surveys follow recognised industry best practice.

  • Visual inspection of all fire-door components
  • Measurement of critical gaps and tolerances
  • Checks on seals, hinges, closers, and hardware
  • High-resolution photographic evidence
  • Priority-coded remedial recommendations

Maintenance & Remedials

Remedial Works

Adjusting door gaps, intumescent seals, and hardware to bring assets back to compliant safety standards.

Preventative

Planned maintenance cycles ensuring your building assets remain safe and functional year-round.

Installations

Supply and installation of certified FD30 and FD60 fire door sets when remediation is not an option.
